# Contributors

The people who have contributed changes to Rosette, in order of first
contribution. Add yourself in your first pull request. The full record is the
commit history: `git shortlog -sne`.

- Jared Hancock (Centered Solutions, LLC)

## AI-assisted contributions

AI tools are not contributors in the copyright sense and are not listed above.
Commits written with an AI assistant credit it with a `Co-Authored-By:`
trailer, and a human contributor signs off on, reviews, and is accountable for
every such commit. See [AI_ASSISTANCE.md](AI_ASSISTANCE.md).
